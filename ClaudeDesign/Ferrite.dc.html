Ferrite · iOS music player

One library, three sources

Thirteen screens at 402×874, plus the icon. Row one covers the core flows; row two covers duplicate resolution, the queue, and the offline state; row three covers the three album types and the three onboarding steps. A single light source per screen sits behind the artwork and fades out, tinted by whatever is playing. Only floating elements use glass: the mini player, the tab bar, the transport button, the action sheet. Lists sit on the background with hairline separators. Every control ships default, hover, pressed, focus and disabled states. Artwork is a striped placeholder; icons are Lucide 0.454 standing in for SF Symbols.
